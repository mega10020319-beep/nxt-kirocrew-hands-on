# Department AI Hands-on Meetup Guide

Our department is hosting a hands-on meetup where students use AI together in practice. Capacity is 12 people, and students who are new to AI practice are welcome to join. The English and Japanese guides are provided only so that you can understand the meetup information — on the day of the meetup, all guidance and hands-on practice will be conducted in Korean. The meetup runs on October 16, 2026, from 18:30 to 20:00, and all times listed are in Korean time (Asia/Seoul). It takes place in the Meeting Room of the Student Union Building, and there is no participation fee. Please bring your own laptop and charger; Wi-Fi is available at the venue. To attend, leave your name in the department group chat by October 14, 2026, 18:00 (Korean time).
